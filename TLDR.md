# Keep Your Gear

Downed pawns keep their weapon and inventory instead of throwing everything on the ground - for colonists and enemies alike, each side with its own options.

## What it does

- Downed pawns - colonists, raiders, animals - keep weapon and inventory until they recover, or die with it on the body.
- A weapon stays equipped when the pawn loses the use of its hands; it is usable again once the pawn can hold things.
- A kidnapped pawn drops its gear where it was grabbed, so it stays recoverable. Pawns that spawn downed - rescued allies, quest survivors - keep their weapon too.
- Separate options for colonists and for other pawns, each covering weapons and inventory, on downed and on death; the "dead" options work standalone in any combination. Stripping, capturing, selling and caravans keep their vanilla behavior.
- A downed enemy who wakes back up is still armed; a downed pawn's kept inventory cannot be accessed directly - recover, strip, capture or kill the pawn to get at it.
